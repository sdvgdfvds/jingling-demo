Sizing notes (board cell ≈ 0.24u):
- Walls: low slab + rubble ≤ ~0.55 cell tall
- Grass blades: 0.16–0.30 cell; no tall bushes
- Water: thin transparent pool + scrolled normals; no foam/glow lights
